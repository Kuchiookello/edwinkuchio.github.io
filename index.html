About Me

Academic formation, international exposure and professional direction.

Professional Profile

I am a multidisciplinary professional with academic formation in philosophy and theology and a developing professional interest in research, analysis, writing, communication and information-based services.


My interests include legal research, legal writing and drafting, research and report writing, critical and analytical thinking, confidentiality and professional ethics, cross-cultural communication, teamwork and collaboration.

Professional Direction

I am interested in opportunities where research, writing, analysis, communication, organisation and problem-solving can be applied in meaningful professional environments.


My international formation has also developed my appreciation for diversity, adaptability and effective collaboration across cultural and linguistic boundaries.

Experience

A journey shaped by service, international formation, academic development and cross-cultural engagement.

My experience has developed through a combination of community service, academic formation and almost a decade of international exposure across different African countries. Living and studying in diverse environments has strengthened my adaptability, communication, independence, teamwork and ability to engage respectfully with people from different cultural backgrounds.
