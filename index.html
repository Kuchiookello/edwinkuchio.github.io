EKO ANALYTICS & RESEARCH

SME Sales & Customer Performance Analysis

A practical business analytics project

01

Project Overview

SME Sales & Customer Performance Analysis is a practical analytics project developed under EKO Analytics & Research. The project demonstrates how business data can be organised and analysed to understand sales performance and customer behaviour.

The purpose is not simply to produce charts, but to translate business information into meaningful insights that can help an SME understand what is working, where performance is changing and where opportunities may exist.

02

Business Questions

How are sales performing over time?
Which products or categories perform best?
Which customers contribute most to revenue?
What customer segments emerge?
Are there geographic or market differences?
Where are the strongest opportunities?
03

Analytical Framework

Data Preparation

Organising, checking and preparing business data for meaningful analysis.

Sales Analysis

Examining revenue, volume, trends and performance across relevant periods.

Customer Analysis

Understanding customer contribution, purchasing behaviour and segmentation.

Performance Insights

Translating analytical findings into practical business observations and recommendations.

04

Key Deliverables

  • Clean and structured analytical dataset
  • Sales performance analysis
  • Customer performance analysis
  • Visual dashboards and charts
  • Key findings and business insights
  • Practical recommendations
05

Professional Value

This project demonstrates the ability to approach a business problem systematically: understand the question, organise information, analyse patterns, communicate findings and connect evidence to practical recommendations.
