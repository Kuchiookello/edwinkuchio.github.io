PROJECT METHODOLOGY

SME Sales & Customer Performance Analysis

The project demonstrates a structured approach to SME business analysis, moving from data preparation to interpretation and practical recommendations.

01. Data Collection

Collect transaction-level information such as customer ID, order date, product, quantity, price, location and sales value.

02. Data Cleaning

Identify missing records, duplicate transactions, inconsistent naming conventions and unusual values.

03. Descriptive Analysis

Calculate sales totals, transaction counts, customer counts, average order values and product contributions.

04. Customer Analysis

Examine new, returning and occasional customers and identify potential retention opportunities.

05. Insight Generation

Translate numerical patterns into understandable observations that can support business decisions.

06. Recommendations

Develop practical actions around customer retention, product performance, market development and KPI monitoring.
